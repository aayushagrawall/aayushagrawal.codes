Attendance Calculator

Enter the classes held and the classes you attended. It works out your attendance and tells you how many you can skip, or how many more you need, to stay at 85%.

What it does

  • Calculates your current attendance
  • Checks it against the 85% rule
  • Shows how many classes you can skip
  • Shows how many you must attend
  • Two inputs, one answer
  • Works for any college or school

How it works

  1. Enter the total number of classes held.
  2. Enter how many of them you attended.
  3. Read your percentage and your skip or attend count.
